No product code, live database, or process was changed by this audit.

**Status meanings:** PROVEN means a source path plus a relevant test or recorded probe supports the stated behavior; it does not imply full release acceptance. INCOMPLETE means a material requested capability or evidence is missing. UNAVAILABLE_BY_PROTOCOL means the current Codex telemetry surface does not provide the requested exact measurement. NEEDS_LIVE_VALIDATION means implementation exists but requires installed Windows runtime/interaction evidence.

This is separate from—and does not satisfy—the natural quota-cycle requirement. Root reports the two fixture identities, Goal budget and usage preserved; the general monitor still does not expose those Goal fields.

No physical restart, tray click, public release, or natural Goal completion is claimed here.
